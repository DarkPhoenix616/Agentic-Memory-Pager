A local chatbot that pages its memory

The context window works like RAM. When it fills past the trigger, the oldest messages are compressed (code by the AST compactor, dialogue by the RLM summarizer) and moved to the vault. Ask about them later and they are paged back in.

Try: tell it a fact, ask a few long questions, then ask about the fact again.
